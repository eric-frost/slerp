### Unscii font

From `crates/retro/font/NOTICE`.

```text
cp437.bin holds the 256 characters of IBM PC code page 437 as 8x16 bitmaps,
cut from Unscii 1.1's unscii-16.hex (http://viznut.fi/unscii/) by
make-font.ts.  Unscii's author wrote "Everything made by me (viznut) is in
the Public Domain" (unscii.txt in the source archive); only the "-full"
variant, which merges in GPL-licensed Unifont glyphs, is not, and it is not
used.  Four glyphs Unscii lacks (the sun, the house, the reversed not sign
and the bullet operator) are derived from other Unscii glyphs or drawn in
make-font.ts.
```
